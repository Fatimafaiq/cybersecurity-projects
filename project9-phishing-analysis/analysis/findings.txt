PROJECT 9 - PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS

Indicators Identified:

1. Suspicious Sender Domain
   support@micros0ft-security.example

   The domain uses "micros0ft" with the number 0 instead of
   the letter "o", indicating a lookalike domain.

2. Suspicious URL
   https://microsoft-login-security.example/verify

   The URL does not use Microsoft's legitimate domain.

3. Urgency
   The subject contains "URGENT" and threatens account
   suspension within 24 hours.

4. Social Engineering
   The recipient is pressured to verify their identity
   immediately.

5. Threatening Language
   The email claims failure to verify could result in
   permanent loss of account access.

ASSESSMENT:
The email contains multiple phishing indicators and should
be treated as malicious/suspicious.

RECOMMENDED ACTION:
Do not click the link or enter credentials. Report the email
to the security/SOC team and investigate the sender and URL.
